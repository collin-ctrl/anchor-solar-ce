What we'll cover

Built by a solar project coordinator who works these deals every week. Practical steps you can use on your next listing or buyer consult.

  1. Solar basics

    Grid-tied, off-grid, and hybrid battery systems in plain English, plus how to read a solar homeowner's utility bill and net metering credits.

  2. Owned vs. leased vs. PPA

    Why ownership decides whether solar is real property, whether it shows up in the appraisal, and what the seller has to pay off or transfer.

  3. Getting solar appraised

    The cost, income, and market approaches, building a PVvalue report, and completing the Residential Green and Energy Efficient Addendum.

  4. Listing a solar home

    O&E reports, the documents to collect from sellers, MLS green fields, and the call to buyer's agents that heads off surprises.

  5. Representing the buyer

    Lease transfers, lender lease letters, supporting the appraisal, and the pre-closing solar handoff.

Solar shouldn't kill the deal

Leased panels a buyer won't assume. An appraiser who gives the system zero value. A payoff nobody saw coming at the title table. Learn to spot each one early and keep your closing on schedule.
